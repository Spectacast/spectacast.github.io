Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data Spectacast handles, why, and what your rights are. It covers the website spectacast.com, the Spectacast customer account, Spectacast Pass, Spectacast Translator, and the licence check in Spectacast Director, Meet and Mic. The phone app Spectacast Mic has its own short policy, linked at the end.

Who we are

Spectacast is the controller of the data described here unless a section says otherwise. Privacy contact: privacy@spectacast.com.

In short

  • Most of Spectacast runs on our customers' own computers. The video, audio and meetings of an event stay there, and we do not receive them.
  • Two parts send data to the internet: Spectacast Pass, which we host, and Spectacast Translator, which sends speech to an outside AI engine.
  • For data about the people at an event (attendees, speakers), the event organiser is the controller and we act as their processor (art. 28 GDPR).
  • We do not sell personal data, show advertising or use tracking cookies.

2. The licence check (Director, Meet, Mic receiver, Translator)

The installed programs contact our licence server when they start and from time to time. They send the licence key, a random identifier for the computer, the program and its version, and the hall it is assigned to. Our server sees the computer's IP address. We use this to check that the plan is active and how many halls are in use (contract, art. 6.1.b) and keep the log for 12 months. The programs also report when they are on air, without any content of the event. When a refund or a payment dispute is opened for your order, we may give Paddle a summary of this log, such as the computers activated and the hours in use, to show whether the plan was used (legitimate interest, art. 6.1.f).

When you press "Send report" in a program, it sends us its logs and versions so we can find a fault. Logs can contain names of devices, meeting participants and hall staff. We use them only for that support case and delete them within 90 days.

3. Spectacast Pass: attendee data

Pass is hosted by us for the event organiser. The organiser is the controller of attendee data and decides what is collected and why; we process it on their behalf, only following their instructions, under a data processing agreement that is part of our Terms for business customers.

Data an organiser typically keeps in Pass:

  • attendee name, email, organisation, category (for example faculty, delegate, staff) and badge code;
  • the times each badge was scanned at each door, and the sessions and time spent in each room computed from them;
  • attendance certificates made from those records;
  • the names of the organiser's staff who use the admin pages and door scanners.

Where it is kept: on servers of Hetzner Online GmbH in the European Union. The door phones keep scans offline until they can send them, then keep only what the scanner screen needs.

How long: the organiser chooses. By default event data is kept for 24 months after the event, which covers continuing medical education (ECM) reporting, and is deleted when the organiser deletes the event or 30 days after their plan ends.

Attendees who want to see, correct or delete their data should ask the event organiser. If they write to us, we pass the request on to the organiser.

4. Spectacast Translator: speech sent to AI engines

Translator listens to a microphone, a hall feed or a meeting and gives back a live translation. To do that, the speech is sent live, over an encrypted connection, to the AI engine chosen for that language:

  • Google (Gemini API), Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC, or
  • OpenAI (Realtime API), OpenAI Ireland Ltd / OpenAI, L.L.C.

The engine returns translated audio and a written transcript. Translator shows the transcript on the operator's screen and keeps it in the program's log on the hall computer; it does not send audio or transcripts to us.

When the plan includes translation minutes, the program uses our engine account: the engine processes the speech as our sub-processor, and we act as processor for the event organiser, who is the controller of the speakers' voices. When the customer uses its own engine keys, the customer contracts with the engine directly and we receive nothing.

What the engines do with it: under their API terms as published on 17 November 2026, neither Google nor OpenAI uses this API data to train its general models. Both keep it for a limited time to detect abuse, then delete it: OpenAI up to 30 days, Google up to 55 days. We keep the optional request logging of our Google project switched off. Their servers may be outside the EU (including the United States); transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Speech can contain personal data, including health information in medical talks (art. 9 GDPR). The organiser must tell speakers that their speech is translated by an outside AI engine, and must not translate speech it has no right to process. The organiser can switch Translator off for any speaker.

5. The website spectacast.com

Our server records each visit's IP address, page, date and browser in its access log, to keep the site secure and working (legitimate interest, art. 6.1.f), and deletes the log after 14 days. The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising. The Pass admin pages store a sign-in token in your browser, which is needed for them to work.

6. Who else receives data

  • Paddle.com (Merchant of Record): orders, payments, invoices, taxes.
  • Hetzner Online GmbH, Germany: hosting of Pass, the licence server and this website.
  • Google and OpenAI: speech to translate, as described above.
  • Porkbun, LLC, United States: forwards email sent to our spectacast.com addresses to our mailbox.
  • Our accountant and lawyers, and public authorities where the law requires it.

Each provider works under a contract that protects the data. A current list of sub-processors is available on request.

7. Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Pass data is on servers in the EU with access limited to the people who run the service, protected by keys, and backed up every night. Phone mics are paired and encrypted end to end with the computer they talk to.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, and to receive it in a portable format. Where we rely on consent you can withdraw it at any time. Write to privacy@spectacast.com: we answer within one month. You can also complain to a data protection supervisory authority, in particular the one where you live or work.

For data in Pass or in a translated session, the event organiser is the controller: write to them first.

9. Children

Spectacast is a tool for businesses and is not directed at children.

10. Changes

If this policy changes we publish the new version here with a new date, and tell customers by email when the change matters.
