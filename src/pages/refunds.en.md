## Consumers in the EU, the EEA and the United Kingdom

If you buy as a consumer, the law gives you the right to withdraw from your purchase within 14 days without giving a reason. Your contract of sale is with Paddle, so you withdraw with Paddle: use the withdrawal option in the Paddle customer portal (the link is in your confirmation email) or go to [paddle.net](https://paddle.net). You can also write to us at {{EMAIL}}, and we pass your statement on to Paddle without delay. For a subscription, this legal right applies to the first payment.

Under Paddle's [Buyer Terms](https://www.paddle.com/legal/checkout-buyer-terms) this legal right ends early if, during those 14 days, you start to download or use what you bought after agreeing at checkout that it is made available to you at once. Our own refund within {{REFUND_DAYS}} days continues to apply in that case, within the limits described above.

You also keep every other right the law gives consumers, for example when a digital product is defective. This policy does not reduce those rights.
