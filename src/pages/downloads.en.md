# Downloads

<p class="lede">The programs for the control room, the hall PCs and the phones. Pass needs no download: it runs in the browser.</p>

{{downloads}}

## What you install

| What | Where | For |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spectacast Setup | Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit | installs Director on the control-room PC; Meet, the hall's OBS setup and Translator on each hall PC; and the Mic receiver |
| Spectacast Mic | Android and iPhone, from the app stores | the phones that talk into the hall |

Setup asks for the PC's role, installs what that role runs, and shows the code that activates the PC in your account. How to go on from there is in [Getting started](/docs/getting-started/).

## Also needed

These are free, and come from their makers:

- [OBS Studio](https://obsproject.com/) on each hall PC
- [NDI Tools](https://ndi.video/tools/) where the PCs exchange video over NDI
