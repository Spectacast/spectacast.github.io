# Account and billing

<p class="lede">Your organisation in Spectacast Account: the people in it, the plan, the PCs that use it, payment and cancelling.</p>

Spectacast Account is at [spectacast.com/account](/account/). One account can belong to several organisations, for example your company and a client's event.

## People and roles

Under **People**, an owner or admin invites someone by email. The link in the email works once, for 7 days; the person chooses a name and a password, or joins with the account they already have.

| Role | Can |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | everything, including managing other owners. An organisation always keeps at least one. |
| Admin | rename the organisation, invite people, change their roles or remove them, except owners |
| Staff | work in the modules |
| Door | only the Pass door phones |

Forgot your password? Use **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page: the link works for one hour. A new password signs out every browser, and under your profile you can sign out a single browser you no longer use.

## Plans and seats

There are four plans: Pass, Director, Show (Director, Meet and Mic) and Complete (all five modules). Each is yearly, or an event pass for one event. See [Pricing](/pricing/) for what each costs.

You buy a plan for a number of halls, called seats:

- Every hall PC takes a seat. The first control-room PC is included; a further one takes a seat.
- For Pass, a seat is a door.
- Seats count per module: a PC gets a module while it is within the seats of the plans that include that module.
- When there are more PCs than seats, the PCs activated first keep theirs.

An **event pass** lasts 7 days from the first day of your event, and works from the day before to the day after, for setting up and taking down.

## Activating a PC

Each PC with Spectacast shows a code after you install it, such as `K7QM-3XRB`. Type it under **Plan and PCs**, **Activate a PC**, within an hour, and say whether it is a hall PC or the control-room PC. The PC then gets its licence and renews it on its own every few hours.

- **Offline:** a paid licence keeps working for 14 days without the internet. For a venue with no internet at all, an owner can download the PC's **Licence file** under **Plan and PCs** and copy it to the PC.
- **A PC you no longer use:** press **Release**. It gets no more licences, and its seat is free for another PC after 24 hours.
- **A new PC:** install, activate, done. A licence works only on the PC it was made for.

## Payment and invoices

Paddle, our reseller, takes the payment and sends the invoice, with VAT where it applies. A business enters its VAT number at checkout.

## Cancelling and refunds

A yearly plan renews each year until you cancel it; an event pass does not renew. You can cancel at any time on [spectacast.com/account/cancel](/account/cancel), without signing in, or in Paddle's portal from the link in your receipt; you get a confirmation by email. The plan stays active until the end of the period already paid; consumers have extra rights after an automatic renewal, set out in the Terms. What you can get back, and when, is in the [Refund Policy](/refunds/) and the [Terms](/terms/).
