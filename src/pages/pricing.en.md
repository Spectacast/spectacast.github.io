## Questions

**What counts as a hall?** One room with its own show computer. One control-room computer is included in every plan; each further control-room computer, such as a backup, counts as one more hall.

**Can I change plan?** Yes. Upgrades apply at once with the difference charged pro rata; more halls can be added at any time.

**How do refunds work?** You can ask for a full refund within {{REFUND_DAYS}} days. See the [Refund Policy]({{REFUND_URL}}).

**Who sends the invoice?** Paddle.com, our reseller and Merchant of Record, charges the payment and sends the invoice with the right taxes for your country.

**Can I buy as a private person?** Yes. A private buyer pays the price plus the VAT of their country. A business that enters a valid VAT number at checkout is charged without VAT where the reverse-charge rule applies.

Installation, on-site crew and show operation are not part of these plans; ask us for a quote.
