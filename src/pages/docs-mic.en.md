# Mic guide

<p class="lede">Phones as wireless microphones and talkback: the Mic receiver on the hall PC turns each phone into an audio input, and sends the hall back to the phones.</p>

## What you need

- The Mic receiver on a Windows PC in the hall. Spectacast Setup installs it with the role **Mic receiver only**.
- The free Spectacast Mic app on each phone, for Android and iPhone, in the app stores from version 1.0.
- The venue's Wi-Fi, with the phones and the PC on the same network. Mic needs no internet.

## Pair a phone

A phone pairs once with each receiver; from then on everything between them is encrypted.

1. On the receiver's control page, press **Pair a phone**. It shows a six-digit code and a QR code for five minutes, for as many phones as you like.
2. On the phone, open the app, tap the receiver, and type the code or scan the QR code.

The control page lists the paired phones. **Remove** takes one off; it then needs a new code.

## Talk

The app shows whether you can be heard and how good the connection is, and has one large button to mute. On the PC, the voices arrive on a microphone input that OBS, the mixer or Meet uses like any other microphone. A PC output goes back to the phones, so they hear the meeting or the hall.

## Several receivers

Receivers can form a group: on one receiver's control page choose **Add a receiver**, which shows a code, and on the other choose **Join another receiver** and type it. A phone paired with one receiver of the group is let in by all of them, and shows each as a tile to switch to. Use one receiver per hall, and one for the crew's talkback with the control room.

## From the control room

In Director the receiver appears on **Devices**. The dashboard shows each phone's level and can mute it. The receiver's control page also has a volume per phone, for one that comes in too loud.

## Good to know

- A phone on speaker in the same room as the hall's loudspeakers is fine: the app cancels the echo.
- Busy Wi-Fi shows on the phone as a weaker connection. A network for the crew, apart from the guests' Wi-Fi, helps.
- The control page tells, for each phone, whether its sound breaks up and on which side, the phone's or the PC's.
