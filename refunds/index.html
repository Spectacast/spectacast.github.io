Refund Policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

Spectacast is sold by our reseller Paddle.com, the Merchant of Record for all our orders. Paddle processes every refund.

Your right to a refund

You can ask for a full refund within 14 days of your purchase. This applies to:

  • a new yearly subscription,
  • each automatic renewal of a yearly subscription, counted from the renewal date,
  • an event pass,
  • translation minutes bought on top of a plan.

The refund goes back to the card or account you paid with, and includes the taxes you paid.

Paddle decides each request under the Paddle Refund Policy. As that policy says, refunds are not issued where there is evidence of fraud, refund abuse or other manipulative behaviour.

How to ask

  • Go to paddle.net, find your order with the email you used, and ask for a refund there; or
  • write to us at support@spectacast.com with your order number, and we will ask Paddle to refund you.

Paddle usually processes a refund within a few working days. Your bank may take longer to show it.

Cancelling a subscription

You can cancel a yearly subscription at any time, from the link in your receipt or at paddle.net. It will not renew, and it stays active until the end of the period already paid. If you cancel more than 14 days after the purchase or renewal, the period already paid is not refunded, except where the law or our Terms give you a right to it.

Consumers in the EU, the EEA and the United Kingdom

If you buy as a consumer, the law gives you the right to withdraw from your purchase within 14 days without giving a reason. Your contract of sale is with Paddle, so you withdraw with Paddle: use the withdrawal option in the Paddle customer portal (the link is in your confirmation email) or go to paddle.net. You can also write to us at support@spectacast.com, and we pass your statement on to Paddle without delay. For a subscription, this legal right applies to the first payment.

Under Paddle's Buyer Terms this legal right ends early if, during those 14 days, you start to download or use what you bought after agreeing at checkout that it is made available to you at once. Our own refund within 14 days continues to apply in that case, within the limits described above.

You also keep every other right the law gives consumers, for example when a digital product is defective. This policy does not reduce those rights.
