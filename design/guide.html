Paper

Spectacast runs live surgery broadcasts. The interface is calm paper and ink, so the one thing that is red, on air, is never missed. Version 0.1.1, chosen 16 November 2026.

Colour

Use the tokens, never a raw hex. Light text colours for ok, warn and live are a shade darker than the mock-ups so small text passes WCAG AA (4.5:1) on paper and white.

Rules

Red means on air

On air, recording, "you are live". Never for delete, errors or warnings: a problem is ink with an icon.

Status has a word

Every state shows a word or an icon. Colour is extra, for people who cannot tell red from green.

Selected is ink

Selected = ink fill with paper text. Director Preview = 2px ink outline. Program live = red fill; the column stays neutral.

Nothing moves

Controls that depend on a mode keep their space (visibility: hidden, not display: none). Fixed column widths; text wraps, columns do not stretch.

Cards stay compact

Actions live in the popup that opens from the card. Small overlay buttons on hover are fine: they float and take no space. Table row actions appear on hover.

Shape

Radius 6 (buttons in dense panels), 10 (tiles, inputs), 18 (cards, dialogs). No brand blue, no gradients.

Lockup

S-stroke mark + "spectacast" + product word in muted weight: spectacast pass. The red dot in the mark is the logo, not a status.

Director

Panels use the console surface (always dark). Colours, type and components may change; panel layout only with Liviu's OK.
