Spectacast Translator

Live translation, as an audio source. Translator listens to a speaker and speaks what they say in another language, on any audio output: the hall's speakers, an interpretation channel of the meeting, or the headsets.

Contact usSee pricing

Any input, any output

Take the signal from a hall microphone, the mixer, a phone through Spectacast Mic, or a hall's program over NDI. Send the translation to a sound card, to the interpreter channel of the meeting through Spectacast Meet, or to the hall. One Translator per language, side by side.

You can read what it says

The control page shows every sentence twice, as heard and as said, so someone who knows both languages can follow along. A check warns when the translation seems to say the opposite of the speaker. With the interpreter engines, a topic and a glossary help it with names and technical terms.

Started from the control room

Director starts and stops each Translator like any other device, and the control page works from any browser on the network.

Engines and minutes

Translator uses an AI speech engine: Google Gemini or OpenAI today, and the engine is a connector. Plans with Translator include 300 minutes per hall; more minutes are billed by use, per minute and language, or you can use your own engine key and pay the engine directly.
