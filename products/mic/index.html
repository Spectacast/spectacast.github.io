Spectacast Mic

Phones as wireless microphones and talkback. Speakers, moderators and the control room talk into the phone they already have, and the hall PC receives each one as an audio device, next to the hall's own microphones.

Contact usSee pricing

A microphone in every pocket

With the free Spectacast Mic app for Android and iPhone, in the app stores from version 1.0, you join the hall's channel and talk. The app shows whether you can be heard and how good the connection is, with one large button to mute. When a question comes from the floor, any phone in the room becomes the microphone.

Talkback for the crew

Channels keep the hall and the crew apart: the stage manager talks to the control room on one channel while the audience hears the speaker on another. Everyone on a channel sees who is talking.

On the PC, just an audio device

The Mic receiver on the hall PC turns each phone into an audio input that OBS, the mixer or the meeting can use like any other microphone, and sends a return so the phones can hear the meeting. Director shows each phone's level and can mute it from the control room.

What it needs

  • The free app on each phone (Android or iOS)
  • A Windows PC or a Mac per hall running the Mic receiver
  • The venue's Wi-Fi: Mic works on the local network, without internet
