Spectacast Pass

Badges, doors and attendance. Every badge carries a QR code; at each door a phone, a tablet or any scanner reads it and answers at once, and afterwards Pass tells you who was in which session, and for how long.

Contact usSee pricing

A door that answers at once

Open the door app on any phone or tablet, or plug a keyboard scanner into a laptop. Each badge gets a full-screen answer in green, amber or red with a sound, and the person's name and category. When the network drops, the door keeps deciding on its own and sends its scans when it is back.

Your event, your rules

Set up the rooms, doors and sessions, the categories of attendees and the rules for each door: who may enter where and when, what the screen says, which sound it plays. Try every rule in the tester before the doors open. Import attendees from a spreadsheet or a registration site, print the badges or email them.

Attendance you can use

Live occupancy shows how full each room is. After the event, Pass gives the minutes and percentage each person spent in every session, ready for Excel, and participants download their certificate of attendance when they meet the requirements you set.

Nothing to install

Pass runs online at spectacast.com, in English, German, Spanish and Italian. The door app works in the browser, and can be added to a phone's home screen.
