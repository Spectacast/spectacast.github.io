Spectacast Meet

The online meeting, as separate video feeds. Meet joins the meeting from a hall PC and gives every participant, the gallery and each screen share their own feed with sound, for the hall to use like a camera.

Contact usSee pricing

Every remote speaker is a source

A surgeon in the operating room, a speaker at home, the slides someone shares: each arrives on the hall's network as its own video and audio feed over NDI. The hall's mixer or OBS picks them like any other camera, and the hall's program goes back into the meeting, so the remote speakers see and hear the room.

Driven from the control room

Director sees who is in each meeting, who is waiting and who is talking. From the dashboard the operator admits people, moves them to the waiting room or the lobby, mutes and unmutes, and places each speaker on the hall's screen, for every hall at once if needed. Rules can do the same on their own: admit the faculty by name, keep the waiting room on, bring everything back after a restart.

Interpretation in the meeting

When the meeting has interpretation channels, Meet can show a clear card for each language and carry the interpreter's audio, including a voice from Spectacast Translator, so listeners pick their language in the meeting as usual.

What it needs

  • A Windows PC per hall, on the venue network with the other PCs
  • A Zoom account that can host the meeting; Zoom is the platform today, and the meeting platform is a connector, so others can follow
  • NDI on the network to carry the feeds to the hall's mixer or OBS
