Getting started

From a new account to your first hall on air: what you need, which PC does what, and the order to set them up.

What you need

  • An organisation in Spectacast Account. Your plans, your PCs and your colleagues live there.
  • A control-room PC with Windows 10 or 11. It runs Director, and the dashboard opens in a browser on it or on a tablet.
  • A PC per hall with Windows 10 or 11, for OBS Studio, Meet and, if you use them, the Mic receiver and Translator.
  • One network for all the PCs. The halls and the control room talk over the venue's own network, so a show keeps going when the internet drops. The online meeting and the translation engines need the internet; the PCs also renew their licence over it at least every 14 days.

Pass needs none of this: it runs online, in the browser, and the doors are phones, tablets or scanners.

Which module runs where

Module Runs on Also needs
Director the control-room PC a browser for the dashboard
Meet each hall PC a Zoom account that can host the meeting
Mic the hall PC, and the phones the free app on each phone, the venue's Wi-Fi
Translator a hall PC or the control-room PC the included minutes, or your own engine key
Pass spectacast.com a phone, tablet or keyboard scanner per door

Set it up in this order

  1. Your account. Sign in, check your organisation's name and invite your colleagues. See Account and billing.
  2. The control-room PC. Download Spectacast Setup, run it and pick Control room. Setup installs Director and shows a code: type it in your account under Plan and PCs within an hour. The PC now has its licence.
  3. Each hall PC. Run Setup on the hall PC and pick Hall PC, then activate it with its code the same way, as a hall PC.
  4. The show. Open the dashboard, start from the example show and connect your first hall. See the Director guide.
  5. The rest. Add Meet, Mic and Translator to the halls that need them, and set up Pass for the doors.

The free trial

The trial starts when your organisation activates its first PC, if it never had a plan. For 30 days you can use every module, with a "Spectacast trial" mark on the pictures:

  • 2 halls and the control-room PC, on air for up to 20 minutes per run
  • 10 minutes of Translator
  • Pass for 1 event with up to 5 attendees

There is one trial per organisation. When you buy a plan, the mark goes and the limits are those of your plan: see Pricing.
