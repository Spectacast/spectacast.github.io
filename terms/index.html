2. What Spectacast is

Spectacast is software for live hybrid events:

  • Spectacast Director: the control room engine and web dashboard that drives the halls, the meeting, the microphones and the video feeds.
  • Spectacast Meet: joins the online meeting and gives each participant and screen share as a separate feed.
  • Spectacast Mic: phones as wireless microphones and talkback. The phone apps are free; the receiver on the PC comes with a plan.
  • Spectacast Translator: live speech translation through an outside AI engine.
  • Spectacast Pass: badges, door scanning and attendance, hosted by us.

Director, Meet, Mic and Translator run on computers you provide. Pass runs on our servers. What each plan includes is shown on the pricing page at the time you buy.
