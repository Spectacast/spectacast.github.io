Live hybrid events

One control room for the hall, the meeting and the mics.

Spectacast runs congresses and shows that happen in a room and online at once: every hall's OBS, the meeting with its remote speakers, the NDI feeds and the microphones, from one dashboard, with rules that take care of the routine.

Modules

Spectacast Director

The control room.

An engine and a web dashboard that see every hall PC, the meeting and the mics at once. Cut scenes, admit and place remote speakers, route audio, and let rules do what an operator would do anyway.

OBSNDIWebRTC monitorWindows · macOS

Spectacast Meet

The meeting, as separate feeds.

Joins the online meeting and gives each participant, the gallery and screen shares their own NDI feed, with the audio. Director controls who goes where.

ZoomNDIOSCWindows · macOS

Spectacast Mic

Phones as microphones.

Speakers and the control room use their phones as wireless mics and talkback. The PC side receives them as audio devices, next to the hall's own mics.
